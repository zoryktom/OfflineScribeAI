SYNTHETIC TEST DATA — NOT REAL PATIENT INFORMATION
====================================================
Purpose: Hypertension / type 2 diabetes follow-up. Tests whether the note
invents medication “control” status or lab values the visit never stated.
Source: written mock dialogue for macOS TTS (Samantha + Fred).
Do not treat names, ages, meds, or vitals as real clinical records.

---

PROVIDER: Thanks for coming in. This is a blood pressure and diabetes follow-up. How have things been?

PATIENT: Okay, I guess. I still take the lisinopril ten milligrams in the morning and the metformin five hundred twice a day. I forget the evening metformin maybe once a week.

PROVIDER: Any dizziness, swelling in your legs, or low blood sugars? Sweating, shakiness, confusion?

PATIENT: No dizziness. Ankles are fine. I don't check sugars at home. I cut back on soda. Still eat rice at dinner.

PROVIDER: Home blood pressure?

PATIENT: Uh, I used the cuff a few times. One thirty something over eighty, I think. I didn't write them down.

PROVIDER: Today's blood pressure is one forty two over eighty eight. Pulse seventy six. Weight is unchanged from last visit. I don't have a new A one C in the chart today, so I won't guess at it.

PATIENT: Is one forty two bad? My brother said his was well controlled on the same pill.

PROVIDER: It's a bit above our usual goal, but not an emergency. Stay on the same lisinopril and metformin for now. I am not changing doses today. Please take the evening metformin even when you feel fine.

PATIENT: Do I need more pills?

PROVIDER: Not today. Let's recheck blood pressure in three months. We'll draw labs then, including an A one C, unless you get lightheaded or your home numbers go over one sixty. Questions about the lisinopril or metformin?

PATIENT: No. I take the morning ones with coffee. Thanks.

PROVIDER: All right. Three month follow-up. Call if you have chest pain, severe headache, or swelling. Take care.
